Korea’s fall lodging coupons drop at 10 a.m. Sept. 22 — what to check first

If you’re planning an October or early-November trip in Korea, a new batch of government-backed lodging discounts opens this morning. Roughly 80,000 coupons will be issued first-come, first-served, but the eligible region matters as much as the headline discount.

01

About 80,000 coupons, starting at 10 a.m.

As part of Korea’s 2026 “Travel in Fall” campaign, the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and Korea Tourism Organization say roughly 80,000 lodging coupons will be released through participating online travel agencies at 10 a.m. on Sept. 22, limited to one per person and available first-come, first-served. The announced stay window runs through Nov. 8.

02

Up to KRW 70,000 outside the capital region

The government’s campaign materials advertise discounts of up to KRW 70,000 for eligible stays outside the Seoul metropolitan area and up to KRW 50,000 for island destinations. Those are maximum amounts, so the actual discount depends on the eligible property and offer shown by the participating booking platform.

03

Choose dates before chasing the coupon

The practical move is to decide which October or early-November weekends actually work for you, shortlist a destination, and then compare the final checkout price across participating platforms. Other fall campaign perks cover transportation, camping and regional travel, but each program has separate rules; don’t assume they automatically stack.
