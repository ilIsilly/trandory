Korea’s free flu shots start Sept. 21: who goes first?

Korea’s national flu vaccination program is rolling out in stages. Here’s the schedule that matters for families living in Korea, including this year’s expanded eligibility for children.

Sept. 21: pregnant people and children who need two doses

The 2026-27 national influenza vaccination program runs from Sept. 21, 2026 through April 30, 2027. The first group includes pregnant people and children ages 6 months through 8 years who need two doses because of their prior vaccination history.

Free shots now cover children through age 14

This season, Korea expanded the child eligibility cutoff from 13 to 14. Children born from Jan. 1, 2012 through Aug. 31, 2026 are covered. Children ages 6 months through 14 who need only one dose can start Sept. 28. Adults 65 and older begin in stages starting Oct. 12, with the oldest age group first.

You can use participating clinics regardless of your address

Eligible patients can receive the vaccine at participating clinics or public health centers regardless of where they live. Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency’s vaccination portal lists participating providers. Calling ahead is sensible because vaccine supply and appointment hours can vary by clinic.

People outside the national program can ask clinics about paid vaccination. Individual medical circumstances can affect vaccination decisions, so check with a clinician when needed.
