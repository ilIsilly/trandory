Driving in Korea over Chuseok? About 9,000 public parking lots will be free

Parking can be one of the annoying parts of a holiday drive. For Chuseok this year, roughly 9,000 parking lots at public institutions, government offices and schools will open free of charge from Sept. 24 through 27 — and you can start finding them in major Korean map apps today.

Trandory ·2026.09.23

Free parking runs Sept. 24–27

Korea’s Ministry of the Interior and Safety says about 9,000 public parking facilities nationwide will be opened free of charge during the four-day Chuseok holiday period. The list includes lots operated by public institutions, government offices and schools.

That does not mean every municipal or public lot is automatically free. Check that a specific facility is on the holiday-opening list before heading there.

Search major map apps starting Sept. 23

Locations will be available from the afternoon of Sept. 23 through the government’s GongU Nuri sharing portal as well as Naver Map, KakaoMap, T map, iNavi Air and Hyundai/Kia navigation systems. Korean search terms such as “추석무료주차장” or “무료공공주차장” are the most direct way to surface the holiday listings.

Check the opening hours, too

Hours and access conditions can vary by facility, especially at schools and public offices. Once you find a lot near your destination, check its listed hours before setting out. Saving a second nearby option is useful in case the first one fills up.
