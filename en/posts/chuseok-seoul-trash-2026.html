In Seoul for Chuseok? Don’t put out the trash on the usual day

Seoul’s household-waste schedule changes over the Chuseok holiday. On Sept. 24, most districts won’t accept trash at all, so it’s worth checking the date before leaving bags outside.

Only three districts accept waste on Sept. 24

According to Seoul’s Sept. 23 holiday guidance, household waste can be put out on Sept. 24 only in Jung-gu, Dongdaemun-gu and Gangnam-gu. In Gangnam-gu, the cutoff is 5 a.m. All other districts prohibit disposal that day. The schedule covers general waste, food waste and recyclables.

Sept. 25 is Gangdong-gu only

On Chuseok Day, Sept. 25, only Gangdong-gu accepts waste. On Sept. 26, service resumes in Yongsan-gu, Dobong-gu and Yeongdeungpo-gu, plus Gwanak-gu from 6 p.m. and Gangnam-gu from 8 p.m. All 25 districts are listed for Sept. 27, though some have neighborhood-specific hours or restrictions.

Check your district before taking bags out

Seoul notes that exact collection days can vary by neighborhood, and disposal is generally after sunset. Check the relevant district website before putting anything outside, especially if you’re staying in an apartment or short-term rental.
