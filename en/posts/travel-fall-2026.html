Planning a fall trip in Korea? Check the October–November deals first

If you have a free weekend but no destination yet, Korea’s new fall travel campaign is worth browsing before you book. The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and the Korea Tourism Organization are running “2026 Travel Fall” across October and November.

Trandory ·2026.09.19
01

The main campaign runs in October and November

On September 15, Korea’s Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism announced a two-month domestic travel campaign with the Korea Tourism Organization. The official VisitKorea domestic travel portal groups offers by transportation, lodging, travel products, region and event.

This is not one blanket discount that applies everywhere. Dates, eligible regions and booking methods vary by program, so it makes more sense to choose the weekends you can travel first and then see what fits.

02

The offers range from local transport to rural stays

Among the nationwide listings currently on the official site is a 50% discount on participating tourist taxis and city tour buses, running through November 30. October listings include 50% off selected small-group rural experiences, while some online-platform rural experiences and stays are scheduled for 30% and 20% discounts respectively during October and November.

If you are looking at November, the official list also includes up to 50% off participating temple stays. Some regional programs have already started in September, though, so there is no single October 1 start date for every offer.

03

Read the booking terms before chasing the biggest percentage

“Up to 50% off” is useful only if the program matches your destination and dates. Eligible products, sales windows and reservation methods differ, and limited programs can close as capacity fills.

A simple approach is to pick two or three possible weekends, filter the official site by region, then check transportation, lodging and activities in that order. A discount is best used as a tiebreaker between places you already want to visit—not as the only reason to travel somewhere.
