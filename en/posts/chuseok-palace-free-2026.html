Visiting Seoul’s palaces over Chuseok? Check this first

A quiet palace walk sounds like a good way to spend the holiday in Seoul. Admission may be free, but tours and reservation-only programs do not all follow the same schedule.

Deoksugung is free September 24–27

According to a September 11 notice from Korea’s Royal Palaces and Tombs Center, Deoksugung will offer free admission for all four days of the Chuseok break, September 24 through 27. The MMCA Deoksugung branch is also free during those dates, though exhibition tours will not run.

Regular palace interpretation follows the holiday timetable. Some Seokjojeon Daehan Empire History Museum tours still require advance reservations, so “free admission” should not be read as “every program is walk-in.”

Jongmyo switches to self-guided visits

A September 16 notice says Jongmyo will temporarily switch from its usual timed-entry format to self-guided visits from September 24 through 27, with free admission. Hours are 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., with last entry at 5 p.m.

From September 24 to 26, foreign-language guided tours will not be offered. Korean tours run at 10 a.m., 11 a.m., 1 p.m., 2 p.m. and 3 p.m. Regular tour operations return on September 27.

Some special programs pause for the holiday

There is one useful catch: Gyeongbokgung’s special Gyeonghoeru and Hyangwonjeong tour does not operate September 24–27. Palace opening hours and reservation-based experiences can have separate calendars. If a specific tour is the reason you are going, check that program’s official listing again before leaving.
