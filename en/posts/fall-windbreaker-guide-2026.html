Why windbreakers make sense again this fall

September can feel warm at lunch and surprisingly breezy on the way home. Here’s how to pick a lightweight jacket you’ll actually keep wearing.

Trandory · 2026.09.14
CONTENTS
  1. Why now
  2. How to choose
  3. How to wear it
01

The windbreaker has moved beyond workout gear

Technical outerwear is having a broader fashion moment in 2026. Vogue has highlighted windbreakers, anoraks and sporty jackets across the season, including brighter windbreakers as a fall trend. The appeal is practical too: they’re light, easy to carry and useful when temperatures swing through the day.

02

If you buy one, start with where you’ll wear it

For commuting, restrained branding, neutral colors and an easy silhouette work with more of a wardrobe. For travel, useful pockets, a hood and adjustable hems matter more. For running, look specifically at weight, ventilation, mobility and visibility rather than assuming a fashion windbreaker will perform like running gear.

One useful distinction: wind resistance and waterproofing are not the same thing. If rain protection matters, check the garment’s actual waterproof or water-repellent specification.

03

Mix technical fabric with clothes you already own

The easiest formula is still a tee, denim and a windbreaker, but it doesn’t have to stop there. A cropped or blouson-shaped jacket can work over a long skirt or dress, while black, navy and cream are easy to carry into a casual office. If you already own neutral outerwear, the brighter color-blocked versions showing up this season can do the opposite job and become the outfit’s focal point.

I’d shop in this order: where will I wear it, what function do I need, what fit feels right, and then what color. That tends to lead to a jacket you’ll reach for after the trend cycle moves on.
