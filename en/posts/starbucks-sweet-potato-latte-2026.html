Starbucks Korea’s new sweet potato latte is here for fall

A new seasonal drink landed in Korean stores today. Here’s what’s actually in it, what launched alongside it, and what’s worth checking before you order.

Trandory · 2026.09.15
CONTENTS
  1. The drink
  2. The fall desserts
  3. Before you order
01

Roasted sweet potato, milk, castella and chewy pearls

Starbucks Korea launched its Castella Sweet Potato Latte nationwide on September 15. It combines a sauce made with roasted sweet potatoes from Muan, South Korea, with milk, then adds castella crumbs and chewy pearl toppings.

Despite “latte” in the name, Starbucks’ launch announcement describes the drink as caffeine-free. That makes it a notably different choice from the coffee-based seasonal lattes already on the fall menu.

02

Chestnut desserts arrived with it

The same seasonal release includes a Marron Mont Blanc Cheesecake and a Marron Madeleine made with Korean chestnuts. A whole Mont Blanc-style cheesecake is also planned for app reservations beginning September 21.

Starbucks Korea also began selling nine Peanuts collaboration items on September 15, designed around an autumn pumpkin-and-foliage theme. If you’re visiting for a specific merchandise item, store inventory is worth checking separately.

03

It’s launch day, so we’re not pretending to know how it tastes

Early product coverage can quickly turn a press release into a “review.” We’re keeping those two things separate. What’s confirmed is the ingredient concept—Muan roasted sweet potato sauce, milk, castella and pearls—and the fact that Starbucks says the drink contains no caffeine.

There’s also a Starbucks Korea Latte Week promotion running September 7–20 for Rewards members. Because coupon eligibility can vary by offer, check the Korean Starbucks app at checkout rather than assuming the new drink qualifies for a particular discount.
