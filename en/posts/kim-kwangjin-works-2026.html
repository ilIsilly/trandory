A guide to Kim Kwang-jin’s songs beyond “Magic Castle”

Kim Kwang-jin is closely associated with The Classic’s “Magic Castle,” but his songwriting credits also connect several major Korean ballads of the 1990s.

Trandory · 2026.09.14

01

He was a composer before “Magic Castle” became his signature

An artist biography published by Korea’s Asia Culture Center dates Kim’s composing debut to 1991 with Han Dong-jun’s “Just Because You Are in This World.” He released his first solo album in 1992 and formed The Classic with Park Yong-jun in 1994, the year the duo released “Magic Castle.”

02

The 2026 live album is an easy way into the catalog

THE PULSE Kim Kwang-jin LIVE 2026, released April 27, 2026, collects 14 songs including “Letter,” “Tokyo Girl,” “Fox,” “Sincerity,” “Do You Know,” and “Magic Castle.” Bugs’ track credits list Kim as composer on all 14 tracks.

03

His melodies also became other singers’ signature songs

Frequently cited examples include Lee So-ra’s “Just Like the First Feeling” and “Remember Me,” Han Dong-jun’s “Promise of Love,” and Lee Seung-hwan’s “To Me” and “Dunk Shot.” KBS archive programming and the Asia Culture Center both identify these among Kim’s songwriting works.

This is a representative guide rather than a complete copyright-registration catalog. But these songs make a strong starting playlist for hearing how one composer’s melodic style traveled across different voices in 1990s Korean pop.
